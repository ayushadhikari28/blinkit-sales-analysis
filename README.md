# BlinkIt Grocery Sales Analysis (Power BI)

Interactive Power BI dashboard analysing BlinkIt grocery sales by item type, fat content, outlet size, location tier and outlet type.

## Dataset
- File: `BlinkIT Grocery Data.xls`
- 8,523 records, 10 outlets, 1,559 unique items, 16 item types
- Source: Kaggle

## Tools
Excel, Power BI Desktop, Power Query, DAX

## KPIs
- Total Sales: 1.2M
- Average Sales: 141.0
- No. of Items: 8,523
- Average Rating: 3.97 / 5

## Key Insights
- Low Fat items give 64.6% of sales vs 35.4% for Regular.
- Fruits and Vegetables (14.8%) and Snack Foods (14.6%) are the top item types.
- Medium outlets lead with 42.3% of sales; Tier 3 locations lead with 39.3%.
- Supermarket Type1 contributes 65.5% of total sales.
- Average sales per item (about 141) and ratings (about 3.9 to 4.0) are nearly the same across all categories, so total sales are driven by volume, not value per item.

## Data Cleaning
Standardised fat content labels (`LF`, `low fat`, `reg`) into `Low Fat` and `Regular`.

## How to Open
Download `blinkit.pbix` from the Blinkit Project folder and open it with Power BI Desktop..
